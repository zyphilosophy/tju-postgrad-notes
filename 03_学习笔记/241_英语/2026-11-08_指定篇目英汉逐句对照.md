## 完形 Passage 8：员工幸福感与身心健康

### 第 1 段

In today’s competitive business environment, employee well-being is one of an organization’s most valuable strategic assets.<br>
在当今竞争激烈的商业环境中，员工的身心健康与幸福感是组织最宝贵的战略资产之一。

The degree to which employees feel **connected** to their work, supported by their teams, and cared for by the organization can significantly shape their well-being, performance, retention, and workplace culture.<br>
员工在多大程度上感到自己与工作紧密相连、得到团队支持和组织关怀，会显著影响他们的身心状态、工作表现、留任情况以及职场文化。

**As a result**, employee well-being has become a central priority for leaders across industries and company sizes.<br>
因此，无论企业属于哪个行业、规模大小如何，员工的身心健康与幸福感都已成为领导者优先关注的核心事项。

Companies that **prioritize** fostering a supportive workplace culture often experience higher productivity, lower burnout rates, and better staff retention.<br>
优先营造支持性职场文化的企业，通常拥有更高的生产效率、更低的职业倦怠发生率和更高的员工留任率。

### 第 2 段

Before exploring why employee well-being matters, it is essential to first define it.<br>
在探讨员工的身心健康与幸福感为何重要之前，首先必须明确这一概念的含义。

In simple terms, employee well-being **refers to** the physical, psychological and social health of employees within a working environment.<br>
简单来说，员工的身心健康与幸福感，是指员工在工作环境中的身体健康、心理健康以及良好的社会交往状态。

Workers with strong well-being are resilient, actively engage with their tasks, and **align** their personal goals with organizational priorities.<br>
身心状态良好、幸福感强的员工更有韧性，会积极投入工作，并使个人目标与组织的重点任务保持一致。

This is not just about job **satisfaction** but a deeper sense of belonging to the team, workplace, and values promoted by the enterprise.<br>
这不仅涉及工作满意度，还涉及对团队、工作场所以及企业倡导的价值观更深层次的归属感和认同感。

A healthy employee does not merely work for a salary.<br>
身心健康的员工并非仅仅为了薪水而工作。

They commit fully to their duties, **claim** ownership of their career development, and feel genuine respect at work.<br>
他们全身心地履行职责，主动掌握自己的职业发展，并在工作中感受到真诚的尊重。

This level of engagement **significantly** improves overall team outcomes.<br>
这种程度的投入会显著改善团队的整体工作成果。

### 第 3 段

One of the central reasons why employee well-being matters is its direct **correlation** with long-term productivity.<br>
员工的身心健康与幸福感之所以重要，一个核心原因在于它与长期生产效率直接相关。

Employees with good well-being are more likely to exceed basic job requirements.<br>
身心状态良好的员工更有可能超越基本工作要求。

Their positive mindset **translates into** higher-quality output, stronger focus, and a greater **willingness** to cooperate with teammates.<br>
他们积极的心态会转化为更高质量的工作成果、更强的专注力以及更强烈的团队合作意愿。

When employees feel valued, they bring **more than** just labor to the workplace.<br>
当员工感到自己受到重视时，他们为工作场所带来的就不仅仅是劳动力。

They share creative ideas, help **solve** workplace conflicts, and often take the initiative to optimize workflows, which lifts the whole team’s performance.<br>
他们会分享富有创意的想法，帮助解决工作中的冲突，并经常主动优化工作流程，从而提升整个团队的表现。

### 第 4 段

High staff turnover can be extremely expensive for companies, both **in terms of** recruitment and training new hires.<br>
员工的高流动率会使企业在招聘和新员工培训两方面都承担极高的成本。

However, employees with good well-being are far less likely to resign, **reducing** the cost of frequent hiring and helping to retain talented staff.<br>
然而，身心状态良好、幸福感强的员工辞职的可能性要小得多，这会减少频繁招聘的成本，并有助于留住优秀人才。

By building a culture that values well-being, organizations can develop a **loyal** workforce ready to stay and grow together with the business.<br>
通过建设重视员工身心健康与幸福感的文化，组织可以培养一支忠诚的员工队伍，使他们愿意留下来，与企业共同成长。

Besides, satisfied employees tend to be **advocates** for their company, helping attract like-minded candidates who share the organizational vision.<br>
此外，满意的员工往往会成为公司的支持者和宣传者，帮助吸引志同道合、认同组织愿景的求职者。

### 第 5 段

Employee well-being also plays an important role in lifting team morale and job satisfaction.<br>
员工的身心健康与幸福感，在提升团队士气和工作满意度方面也发挥着重要作用。

When workers feel respected, safe, and connected to colleagues, it exerts a positive influence on their mental and physical state.<br>
当员工感到受到尊重、拥有安全感，并与同事关系紧密时，这会对他们的身心状态产生积极影响。

This, **in turn**, builds a harmonious working atmosphere, strengthens bonds among teammates, and creates a more rewarding professional experience.<br>
这进而营造出和谐的工作氛围，加强团队成员之间的联系，并带来更有收获的职业体验。

In contrast, employees with poor well-being often feel exhausted, anxious or disconnected, which may **lead to** low efficiency and a negative workplace climate.<br>
相比之下，身心状态不佳、幸福感低的员工往往感到疲惫、焦虑或疏离，这可能导致工作效率低下，并形成消极的职场氛围。

Attention to well-being helps mitigate these risks and cultivates a **sense** of belonging within the team.<br>
关注员工的身心健康与幸福感，有助于降低这些风险，并在团队内部培养归属感。

### 第 6 段

Understanding and supporting employee well-being is therefore essential for companies aiming to build a stable, high-performing workforce that achieves lasting development.<br>
因此，对于希望建立稳定、高绩效员工队伍并实现长远发展的企业而言，理解员工的身心健康与幸福感，并为之提供支持，至关重要。

## Part 2 · Passage 5：机器人技术与自动化

### 第 1 段

Robotics and automation are no longer confined to science fiction or large factories.<br>
机器人技术和自动化已经不再局限于科幻作品或大型工厂。

They now appear in warehouses, hospitals, farms, offices, and even homes.<br>
如今，它们已经出现在仓库、医院、农场、办公室，甚至家庭中。

From self-driving vehicles to software that processes invoices, automated systems are changing how work is done.<br>
从自动驾驶汽车到处理发票的软件，自动化系统正在改变工作的完成方式。

This shift raises important questions about productivity, employment, safety, and the future of human labor.<br>
这一转变引发了有关生产效率、就业、安全以及人类劳动未来的重要问题。

### 第 2 段

Robotics and automation are related but not identical.<br>
机器人技术与自动化相互关联，但并不完全相同。

A robot is a programmable machine designed to carry out physical tasks, often with sensors and moving parts.<br>
机器人是一种可编程的机器，用于执行实体操作任务，通常配有传感器和活动部件。

Automation is a broader concept: it refers to technology that performs tasks with minimal human input.<br>
自动化是一个范围更广的概念，指只需极少人工参与就能执行任务的技术。

Therefore, a robot is often a form of automation, but automation can also be purely software-based, such as a system that schedules appointments or detects fraud.<br>
因此，机器人通常是自动化的一种实现形式，但自动化也可以完全基于软件，例如安排预约或检测欺诈行为的系统。

Understanding this distinction helps clarify both the opportunities and the challenges these technologies create.<br>
理解这一区别，有助于明确这些技术带来的机遇与挑战。

### 第 3 段

The benefits of automation are significant.<br>
自动化带来的益处十分显著。

Machines can work with high precision, consistency, and speed, and they do not become tired or distracted.<br>
机器能够以高精度、稳定一致的表现和高速度工作，而且不会疲劳或分心。

This makes them especially useful for dangerous, repetitive, or physically demanding tasks such as lifting heavy materials, inspecting equipment, or handling hazardous chemicals.<br>
这使机器特别适合执行危险、重复性强或体力要求高的任务，例如搬运重物、检查设备或处理危险化学品。

Automation can also improve quality control, reduce waste, and free human workers to focus on more complex activities that require judgment, creativity, and communication.<br>
自动化还能够改善质量控制、减少浪费，并使劳动者腾出精力，专注于需要判断力、创造力和沟通能力的更复杂的活动。

In many industries, these improvements can lower costs and increase competitiveness.<br>
在许多行业中，这些改进能够降低成本并增强竞争力。

### 第 4 段

However, the economic and social effects are not automatically positive.<br>
然而，其经济和社会影响并不必然是积极的。

Automation can displace workers whose jobs consist mainly of routine tasks.<br>
自动化可能使那些主要从事常规性任务的劳动者失去原有岗位。

Yet the outcome is not simply “machines replace people.”<br>
但结果并非简单的“机器取代人”。

Many jobs change rather than disappear, as workers take on new responsibilities involving supervision, maintenance, programming, or data analysis.<br>
许多工作岗位发生的是转变，而非消失，因为劳动者会承担涉及监督、维护、编程或数据分析的新职责。

This transition requires investment in training and education.<br>
这一转型需要对培训和教育进行投入。

Without effective reskilling programs, displaced workers may struggle to find new opportunities, and inequality may widen.<br>
如果没有有效的再技能培训项目，失去原有岗位的劳动者可能难以找到新的机会，不平等也可能加剧。

Companies and governments therefore need to plan carefully for workforce transitions.<br>
因此，企业和政府需要为劳动力转型作出周密规划。

### 第 5 段

There are also practical and ethical challenges.<br>
此外，还存在现实层面和伦理层面的挑战。

Automated systems can be expensive to purchase and maintain, which may disadvantage small businesses.<br>
自动化系统的购置和维护成本可能很高，这可能使小企业处于不利地位。

They can introduce cybersecurity risks and safety concerns, especially when robots work alongside people.<br>
这些系统可能带来网络安全风险和作业安全问题，尤其是在机器人与人共同工作时。

In addition, questions of accountability arise when an automated decision causes harm: who is responsible—the manufacturer, the operator, or the programmer?<br>
此外，当自动化决策造成损害时，就会出现责任归属问题：究竟应由制造商、操作人员，还是程序员负责？

These issues mean that adoption must be guided by clear regulations, robust testing, and transparent decision-making.<br>
这些问题意味着，采用自动化技术必须以明确的法规、严格可靠的测试和透明的决策为指导。

### 第 6 段

The future of robotics and automation is likely to involve greater collaboration between humans and machines.<br>
机器人技术和自动化的未来，很可能涉及人类与机器之间更广泛的协作。

Collaborative robots, or “cobots,” are designed to work safely alongside people, while artificial intelligence enables systems to adapt to changing environments.<br>
协作机器人，即“cobots”，旨在与人安全地并肩工作；人工智能则使系统能够适应不断变化的环境。

The goal should not be to replace human workers wherever possible, but to augment their capabilities, improve working conditions, and create sustainable value.<br>
目标不应是尽可能地取代人类劳动者，而应是增强他们的能力、改善工作条件，并创造可持续的价值。

Ultimately, the success of robotics and automation will depend less on the technology itself and more on how thoughtfully societies choose to adopt and govern it.<br>
最终，机器人技术和自动化能否成功，与其说取决于技术本身，不如说更取决于社会在选择如何采用和治理这些技术时是否深思熟虑。

## Part 2 · Passage 6：终身学习

### 第 1 段

Lifelong learning is no longer a luxury but a necessity in today's rapidly changing world.<br>
在当今快速变化的世界中，终身学习已不再是奢侈的选择，而是一种必需。

It refers to the continuous, voluntary, and self-motivated pursuit of knowledge for personal or professional reasons.<br>
它是指出于个人或职业方面的原因，持续、自愿并由自身动力驱动地追求知识。

Unlike formal education, which is often confined to childhood and early adulthood, lifelong learning spans one's entire life and takes place in various settings—classrooms, workplaces, online platforms, and everyday experiences.<br>
正规教育往往局限于童年和成年早期；与之不同，终身学习贯穿人的一生，并发生在各种场景中，包括课堂、工作场所、在线平台和日常经历。

In a knowledge-based economy, the ability to learn continuously has become a core survival skill.<br>
在知识经济中，持续学习的能力已成为一项核心生存技能。

### 第 2 段

The primary driver of lifelong learning is the accelerating pace of change.<br>
推动终身学习的主要动力，是不断加快的变化速度。

Technologies evolve, industries transform, and job requirements shift constantly.<br>
技术在演进，产业在转型，工作要求也在不断变化。

A skill that was valuable a decade ago may be obsolete today.<br>
一项十年前很有价值的技能，如今可能已经过时。

To remain employable and adaptable, individuals must continually update their knowledge and acquire new competencies.<br>
为了保持就业能力和适应能力，个人必须不断更新知识并掌握新的技能与能力。

Beyond economics, lifelong learning enriches personal life.<br>
除了经济层面的作用外，终身学习还能丰富个人生活。

It fosters curiosity, critical thinking, and creativity, enabling people to understand the world more deeply and engage with it more meaningfully.<br>
它培养好奇心、批判性思维和创造力，使人们能够更深入地理解世界，并以更有意义的方式参与其中。

Learning a new language, mastering a musical instrument, or exploring a new subject can bring joy and a sense of accomplishment, contributing to mental well-being and social connection.<br>
学习一门新语言、掌握一种乐器，或探索一个新学科，都能带来快乐和成就感，有助于心理健康并增进社会联系。

For older adults, learning can also help maintain cognitive health and reduce isolation.<br>
对于老年人而言，学习还有助于保持认知健康，减少孤独和社会隔离。

In addition, lifelong learning promotes intercultural understanding and empathy by exposing learners to diverse perspectives.<br>
此外，终身学习使学习者接触多样的观点，从而增进跨文化理解与共情能力。

### 第 3 段

However, lifelong learning faces obstacles.<br>
然而，终身学习也面临障碍。

Time constraints, financial costs, and lack of motivation are common barriers.<br>
时间限制、经济成本和动力不足是常见的障碍。

Many adults struggle to balance work, family, and study.<br>
许多成年人很难兼顾工作、家庭和学习。

Moreover, traditional education systems often fail to instill a love of learning; instead, they may emphasize grades over curiosity, leaving graduates relieved to close their textbooks rather than eager to open new ones.<br>
此外，传统教育体系往往未能培养人们对学习的热爱，反而可能更重视分数而非好奇心，结果使毕业生在合上课本时如释重负，而不是渴望打开新的书本。

To overcome these challenges, both individuals and societies must act.<br>
为了克服这些挑战，个人和社会都必须采取行动。

Individuals need to cultivate a growth mindset—believing that abilities can be developed through dedication and effort.<br>
个人需要培养成长型思维，即相信能力可以通过投入和努力得到发展。

They should also set aside regular time for learning, even if only a few hours each week.<br>
他们还应定期留出时间学习，即使每周只有几个小时。

Societies, meanwhile, should provide accessible learning opportunities, such as free online courses, community workshops, and employer-supported training.<br>
与此同时，社会应提供易于获得的学习机会，例如免费的在线课程、社区研习班以及雇主支持的培训。

Flexible learning formats, such as micro-credentials and mobile apps, can make study more manageable for busy adults.<br>
灵活的学习形式，例如微证书课程和移动应用，可以使忙碌的成年人更容易安排和完成学习。

Governments can offer tax incentives or subsidies for adult education, and employers can build learning into job roles rather than treating it as an afterthought.<br>
政府可以为成人教育提供税收优惠或补贴，雇主也可以把学习纳入岗位职责，而不是把它当作事后才考虑的附加事项。

### 第 4 段

Lifelong learning is essential for personal fulfillment and professional success in a dynamic world.<br>
在不断变化的世界中，终身学习对于实现个人价值和取得职业成功至关重要。

It is not merely about acquiring skills but about nurturing a mindset of continuous growth and a more fulfilling life.<br>
终身学习不仅是获取技能，更在于培养持续成长的心态，并使生活更加充实。

The journey of learning never truly ends; it is a lifelong adventure that enriches every stage of life.<br>
学习的旅程永远不会真正结束；它是一场贯穿一生的探索，使人生的每个阶段都更加丰富。

工程师运用科学原理，设计各种结构、机器和产品。

他们寻求更好地利用现有资源的方法，并经常开发新材料。

## Part 3 · 第 3 篇：绿色发展

Green development means taking from nature at the proper time and to the proper extent.<br>
绿色发展意味着向自然索取要取之有时、用之有度。

To this end, we as human beings have to regulate our behavior, correct our wrongdoings, and abandon the old idea of utilizing, conquering and transforming nature in favor of respecting, accommodating and protecting nature.<br>
为此，人类必须规范自身行为，纠正错误做法，摒弃利用自然、征服自然、改造自然的旧观念，转而尊重自然、顺应自然、保护自然。

我们必须改变以大规模生产、大规模消费和大量排放为特征的传统模式，将经济活动和人类活动控制在自然资源与生态环境的承载能力范围之内，从而为自然恢复留出时间和空间。

Most of the work that most people have to do is not that interesting, but even such work has certain great advantages.<br>
大多数人不得不做的工作，大多并没有那么有趣，但即使这样的工作，也有一些很大的好处。

首先，工作占据了一天中的许多时间，使人无须考虑自己该做些什么。

当大多数人可以按自己的意愿自由安排时间时，他们往往不知所措，想不出什么足够令人愉快、值得去做的事情。

而且，无论他们最终决定做什么，总会觉得做别的事情本来可能更愉快，并为此感到烦恼。

能够明智地安排闲暇，是文明发展到较高阶段的产物，而目前很少有人达到这一水平。

此外，作出选择这件事本身就令人疲惫。

除了那些特别有主见、富有主动性的人之外，只要安排的任务不太令人厌烦，人们其实很乐意被告知一天中的每个时段该做些什么。
